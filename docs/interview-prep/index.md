# Interview Prep

Everything on this site feeds into this section. Here the material is organized the way interviews actually happen: by difficulty, by scenario, and by how much time you have left before the call.

## Question Bank

Questions are collapsible so you can self-test: read the question, answer out loud, then expand to compare. Each answer notes what strong candidates add and the likely follow-up.

- **[Junior Questions](questions-junior.md)**: fundamentals every Angular developer is expected to know cold.
- **[Mid-Level Questions](questions-mid.md)**: operators, patterns, and the choices interviewers probe hardest.
- **[Senior Questions](questions-senior.md)**: semantics, architecture, and failure modes that separate levels.

## Practice

- **[Scenarios](scenarios.md)**: feature descriptions with the requirement → criterion → operator reasoning interviewers score.
- **[Coding Challenges](coding-challenges.md)**: write-the-pipeline exercises with hidden solutions, sized for live-coding rounds.
- **[Common Traps](common-traps.md)**: fourteen misconceptions that fail interviews, each corrected in two lines.

## Fast Revision

- **[Operator Cheat Sheet](cheat-sheet-operators.md)**: every decision table on one page: "I want to X" → operator.
- **[60-Minute Revision](sixty-minute-revision.md)**: a timed plan for the final hour before the interview.

## How to Use This Section

- **Weeks out:** work through the [Learn path](../learn/index.md) and operator pages; every page ends with its own Q&A.
- **Days out:** drill the question bank top to bottom at your target level, plus one level up, then the [scenarios](scenarios.md) and [challenges](coding-challenges.md) with solutions covered.
- **The final hour:** the [60-minute plan](sixty-minute-revision.md), nothing else.
- **Interviewers:** the bank doubles as a question source; answers describe what a strong response includes.
